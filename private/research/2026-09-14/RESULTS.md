## PC: a relevant sale report changes the priority

eBay Product Research reports one unit of [item 298567238585](https://www.ebay.de/itm/298567238585) sold on 25 August 2026 for **€522.30**, with €24.10 reported shipping. The original German private-seller listing describes an i7-11700F, RTX 3060 12 GB, 32 GB RAM and 1 TB SSD plus 4 TB HDD. Its H510M PRO motherboard is particularly relevant. Compared with the target's seller claims, RAM and storage are greater and the specified PSU differs: 750 W Gold versus 500 W Bronze.

The original page displays a crossed-out **€549** and an accepted-offer marker; €549 is not treated as the sale price. Its €23.99 shipping differs from the research table. The captured sources retain both discrepancies. The sale remains **source-reported**, without settlement records. Four photos show exterior angles and a powered tower with a visible graphics card; they do not establish every specification or stable operation. Six gallery photos were not inspected.

The reported item price is **€77.70 below the target's €600 ask**, before travel, testing, selling expenses or compensation for work. This single comparison supports lowering the case's research priority, not a precise valuation, maximum bid or universal price ceiling. Different case, component brands, seller presentation, channel and unverified physical condition remain material. Two additional research rows use the different RTX 3060 Ti GPU and were not audited as direct comparables: [168623127241](https://www.ebay.de/itm/168623127241), €677.10, 19 August; [227485455499](https://www.ebay.de/itm/227485455499), €422.14, 28 August.

The [official MSI German specification](https://storage-asset.msi.com/specSheet/de/desktop/MAG%20Infinite%2011TC-1221DE.pdf) documents an Infinite 11TC variant with i7-11700F, H510, RTX 3060 12 GB, 16 GB RAM and 1 TB SSD plus 1 TB HDD. Thus “H510” versus “Infinite 11TC” does not alone establish an identity contradiction. It also lists Windows 10 with an upgrade path to 11. This establishes plausibility, not the target's exact SKU or installed parts.

## What would allow the cases to advance

For the PC, only revisit the current ask if there is materially different bundle evidence or a lower actual offer. Before any acquisition decision, verify exact components, included monitor/accessories, present load-test stability and storage health; establish pickup, preparation and selling costs. No unknown cost is zero.

For the car, an inspection must locate the oil leak, diagnose the AC fault and produce a written parts-and-labour quote. Check cold start, a permitted road test, engine/underbody condition, odometer, VIN and documents, HU report, service/timing-belt evidence and the complete seven-seat configuration. Inspection, transport, preparation, repairs, selling costs and operator work remain unpriced. A generic web repair allowance would conceal the decisive uncertainty. See INSPECTION.md for the prepared field checklist.

The remaining physical evidence requires access to the assets and a person able to inspect them. No seller contact or inspection booking has been made. More directly comparable completed transactions would also be needed for a supported vehicle valuation. Today's evidence does not make either purchase ready.
