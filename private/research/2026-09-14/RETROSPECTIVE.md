Recorded 14 September 2026 after the user's request for critical analysis and documentation. This is the owner-authorized public derivative of a local operating retrospective and scope correction. It supplements the retained reports; it does not rewrite their evidence or retrospectively change their acceptance results.

Completion of bounded research and a successful independent review do not complete the broader daily-workflow request. ## Attribution: code versus research execution

The implementation inventory lists seven changed baseline files concerning documentation, packaging/dependencies and distribution verification, plus 73 added release files containing the separate operations package, resources and tests. It lists no changed baseline collector, evaluator or role-template source. A fresh read-only hash comparison during the critical analysis examined 36 .py/.md/.json files under collector/, pilot/, evaluation/ and resale_tool/ against the retained pre-implementation baseline: zero differences. The packaged REVIEWER 3.2, dated 13 September, was already in that baseline. It was not introduced by today's operations implementation.

The observed PC gain followed a configuration query instead of the earlier exact chassis query. The vehicle gain followed broader wording, a correctly applied whole-vehicle category, verified used condition and browser recovery. Those are coordinator research actions. They are plausible explanations for improved retrieval; the data do not isolate their individual contributions or establish that the operations code caused them.

The inspected comparison sales predate yesterday's runs. This suggests missed historical evidence rather than a newly emerged set of sales. Their availability in yesterday's search index was not verified, so the counterfactual remains qualified.

Yesterday's frozen protocols also already required several sound practices: raw retention, verified filters and separation of sold observations from asking amounts; version 2.7 included configuration-based PC queries and bounded recovery. Some of today's improvement therefore repairs execution weaknesses already identified by yesterday's audits. It cannot all be credited to newly invented safeguards. The older PC protocol's restrictive fallback also limits comparison with today's more adaptive approach.

The new code did execute in the offline reassessment: upstream binding around the existing economic evaluator, evidence adaptation, source/cost-policy checks and manual readiness composition. However, several assessments deliberately received missing records or unknown values. An unresolved result from such inputs is evidence of missing-input handling, not independent proof that permission was denied, resources were unavailable, or yesterday's inspection was omitted. Full operations.service assessment was not executed.

## Decision value and remaining weaknesses

For PC 3510971083, the retained €600 ask exceeds the relevant eBay-reported €522.30 item price by €77.70 before costs. The original comparable shows an accepted offer against a displayed €549 and a shipping amount that differs from Product Research. Both sources are retained separately. More RAM/storage in that comparable makes deprioritization reasonable, but one partially audited sale report does not establish a precise target value, maximum bid, general price ceiling or impossible resale. Neither settlement nor current target function is independently established.

For Berlingo 3511992820, the original-sale inspections identified real differences: a five-seat engine-damaged diesel and a two-seat accident vehicle cannot directly price the target's seven-seat configuration with undiagnosed oil loss and failed AC. Research/original price discrepancies remain explicit. Asking prices and damaged-vehicle observations do not establish a repair allowance, achievable resale price or salvage floor. Physical diagnosis, a written repair quote and stronger matching transactions remain necessary for valuation. They do not prevent research on other listings.

The observed gains are therefore improved research relevance, retained evidence and a more useful PC priority judgment. There is no demonstrated reduction in false-positive acquisitions: yesterday already abstained. There is also no measured increase in actionable listings, better price accuracy, lower cost or faster completion. The extensive offline test pass verifies software behavior, not market usefulness or profitable operation.

## Fairness, effort and scope failures

This was not a controlled old-versus-new comparison. The same targets help, but the operator/role changed; today's coordinator had visible asking prices and hindsight from yesterday's failures; queries, filters, periods and recovery effort differed; only two purposively selected cases were examined. Today's PC controls also conflicted: a 180-day URL request and last-year button accompanied a displayed 16 August–14 September result period. Vehicle results covered a year, with four of eight row dates within 180 days. Neither search establishes uniform coverage across cases.

No matched runtime/token/cost accounting covers both complete workflows. Browser retries, repeated large state reads, supervision and review add effort; there is no measured saving to report. The first vehicle tab misapplied its category and repeatedly timed out before a fresh background tab succeeded. That recovery worked in this instance, without proving a general browser cause or permanent fix.

The main service failure was allowing implementation verification and checks on the same cases to displace fresh discovery. Additional safety/readiness checks did not require that omission. A qualified automated worker boundary remains unfinished, but the existing supervised manual research path was used successfully; that automation dependency was not a reason to stop all discovery. The coordinator also needed to distinguish an inspection requirement for one candidate from a blocker affecting the whole workflow.
