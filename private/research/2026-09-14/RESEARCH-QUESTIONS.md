# Open research agenda

These are investigation options, not established requirements or completed studies. Select according to the current assignment, decision value and available evidence. The project supports broad technical, methodological, operational and market research; do not restrict every task to the two historical examples.

| Area | Decision to inform | Useful next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery coverage and selection | Can bounded searches produce a defensible set of candidates without confusing unreviewed listings with rejections? | Search-window coverage, caps, duplicate/time-boundary handling, shortlist rationale and consecutive outcomes. Fresh live collection needs the appropriate assignment and source access. |
| Comparable retrieval | Which query/filter/recovery practices find relevant evidence at reasonable effort? | Fixed-case comparisons with equal budgets; genuine originals, mismatch exclusions, retained dates/filters and full effort. The two-case retrospective is a lead, not causal proof. |
| Evidence usefulness and abstention | Which missing facts are decision-critical, and which checks add little practical value? | Value-of-information analysis, cases where evidence changes priority/valuation, uncertainty decomposition and simpler alternatives. Do not weaken evidence standards merely to increase positive decisions. |
| Valuation and uncertainty | How should verified transactions, variant/condition/service differences and sparse evidence affect estimates? | Independent price/outcome labels, explicit transfer assumptions, lineage-aware holdouts and calibrated uncertainty. Asking prices and modelled estimates remain separately identified. |
| Inspection and repair | What evidence can bound condition risk economically for each product category? | Category-specific protocols, diagnostic discrimination, tool availability, actual quotes, failed inspections and repair outcomes. Do not generalize the business-desktop protocol to cars or gaming systems without review. |
| Economics and operating feasibility | Which costs, work and resource constraints dominate realized contribution? | Dated/source-backed fees, measured effort, inspection/transport/repair costs, holding time, no-purchase leads and follow-up. Unknown costs are not free inputs. |
| Source and evidence access | Which sources establish transactions, conditions and market relevance under usable access terms? | Current primary documentation, geography/account/route constraints, price semantics and reproducible authorized evidence capture. A marketplace name alone is insufficient. |
| Isolated execution | What is the simplest enforceable worker design that satisfies the actual boundary? | Compare viable hosts and threat models; demonstrate forbidden reads/network, cancellation, crashes and truthful metering before integration. No infrastructure installation is implied by a research assignment. |
| Human decisions and experiments | Can research depth, documentation or advice order improve decisions without biased evaluation? | Independent labels, participant/exposure controls, comparable budgets, prospective endpoints and failure/attrition reporting. A benchmark API is not a completed study. |
| Workflow usability and maintenance | Can a researcher/operator reliably complete the useful path without excessive ceremony? | Independent onboarding, failure recovery, duplicated/manual work, decision latency and targeted simplification; check existing implementations first. |

For each investigated area, identify what already exists, the strongest competing explanations, the smallest informative test, dependencies, expected effort and a stopping rule. Prefer findings that change a concrete decision. Record insufficient evidence as such; avoid inventing income goals or profitable operation from software tests.
