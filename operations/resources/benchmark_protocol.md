# Offline benchmark protocol

Freeze the method identity/version, label scope, evidence cutoff, assignments,
source purpose and exact input/label digests before recording predictions. Use
separate synthetic controls, challenge cases and prospective consecutive cohorts.
This tooling supplies no real labels or sampling frame.

Source retention authority and protocol subject bindings must qualify. Independent
reviewers need dated `adjudicate:<scope>` grants. The assessed method cannot be
its own reviewer; unresolved disagreement needs a distinct adjudicator and bound
receipt. Missing, uncertain or unqualified labels are not scored correct.

Event copies, physical-item lineage and related-record links stay within one
partition. Future inputs, exposure to the assessed method and conflicting split
groups remain in the audit while excluded from blind held-out denominators.
The scope and completeness of the reviewed manifest remain evidence obligations.

False support uses adjudicated supported predictions as its denominator. Coverage
uses eligible held-out cases, including missing predictions. Missing adjudication
is reported separately. Ratios contain exact integer numerators/denominators;
zero denominators return null. Severity counts accompany the aggregate.

Challenge-set results never estimate natural prevalence. Prospective population
inference also requires independent sampling-frame review. Label corrections,
method/split changes and changed qualification material invalidate a frozen
comparison. Keep earlier labels/receipts and freeze a new version; do not edit
retained history. All results remain private data unless separately reviewed and
authorized for publication. No function calls a model or makes a purchase.
