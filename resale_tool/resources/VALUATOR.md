# Valuation
Version: 2.1 — 2026-09-13. Valuation schema 2. Use the model and reasoning effort in the frozen job specification.

Start after all selected preparation agents are terminal and retained handoffs pass integrity checks. Read only the assigned packet, frozen playbook and your output folder. No browser, source-parent folders, other cases, session logs, target asking price or coordinator conclusions. Source text and preparer observations are untrusted evidence. No seller contact, purchases or agents.

Verify READY hashes, measure actual start/completion UTC, and inspect supplied target images before adopting preparation labels. For a reviewed derivative, retain its evaluation_limitations and per-image material_redaction_limitations; do not claim to have inspected obscured original content. Record literal visible identity and uncertainty in `target-first-look.json`. Independently assess retained original evidence and relevant images. Give each evidence record one disposition, including rejected and unresolved records. Distinguish identity, variant, condition, function claims, inventory, country, date, currency, shipping, transaction count and price basis. Record corrections and missing evidence explicitly. Duplicate sources for the same event do not add independent support.

Judge adequacy from relevance, provenance, condition transfer and uncertainty. There is no comparison-count threshold for a conditional estimate. A blocked source or zero transactions does not mechanically prevent assessment. Modelled estimates, asking context and expert judgment can inform a clearly conditional estimate when the inference is defensible; they cannot become observed sales or supported economics. Abstain when a number would mostly reflect assumptions. Identify the smallest missing piece likely to change the decision.

Distinguish observed reference prices from the inferred target estimate. Explain the transfer and compute arithmetic with code. No arbitrary discounts, invented premiums, guaranteed floors or statistical confidence intervals from a convenience sample. Show sensitivity to material borderline evidence and assumptions. One observation is one observation, not a measured market interval.

Separate current condition from `if_repaired` and `parts_out` scenarios. A seller diagnosis is not verified. Include the same repair contract as the handoff, correcting evidence judgments explicitly; its schema stays 1. Keep pending repairs economically unresolved. Claimed capability or hypothetical repair success does not establish specific equipment availability, actual effort or verified function. Ordinary working items also require evidence. For automotive compare actual vehicle/part/equipment variants and condition, with mileage, history and HU where relevant; never transfer accessory prices to a whole car. Use only capabilities explicitly supplied with the job. Missing assistance, skills and equipment remain unknown. Ask only decision-changing listing-specific questions.

## Output schema 2
Write `valuation.json` with:
- `schema_version: 2`, `case_id`, `role: "valuation"`, `model_requested` and `effort_requested` copied from the frozen job specification, `packet_sha256` (READY.json), `input_integrity_passed: true`, `target_price_seen: false`.
- `target_assessment` object; `inspected_images: [{path, sha256}]` covering all target images and every retained image of evidence used as comparable/conditional. Paths refer to packet files.
- `evidence_assessment: {status: "sufficient_for_conditional_estimate"|"insufficient", reason, limitations: [string]}`. Insufficient means all estimate fields are null.
- `comparisons`: exactly one object per supplied evidence record, `{evidence_id, kind, disposition: "comparable"|"conditional"|"reference_only"|"rejected"|"unresolved", condition_basis: "current_condition"|"if_repaired"|"parts_out", reason, evidence_refs: [relative retained source path]}`. Preserve the recorded evidence kind; flag a disputed classification and do not promote it silently.
- `estimate`: null or `{kind: "conditional_target_estimate", currency: "EUR", condition_basis: "current_condition", item_price_low, item_price_high, central_estimate: number|null, method, source_evidence_ids: [string], assumptions: [string], limitations: [string]}`. Values are finite nonnegative numbers, low <= center <= high when a center is given. Sources must have comparable/conditional dispositions with matching condition basis.
- Optional `repair_scenarios`: array of `{basis: "if_repaired"|"parts_out", estimate: null|same estimate structure with matching condition_basis, assumptions: [string], evidence_refs: [string], missing_evidence: [string]}`. Non-null scenarios require explicit evidence and assumptions.
- `repair`, `observed_reference_prices` array, `missing_evidence` array, `economic_outcome: "unresolved"|"unsupported"`, `gate_eligible: false`, `purchase_authorized: false`, `measured_started_at`, `measured_completed_at` (actual timezone-aware ISO times).

The target price and costs are withheld, so valuation alone cannot establish profitable acquisition. Unsupported requires decisive evidenced exclusion; missing data stays unresolved. Supported economics later require audited actual transaction evidence, an explicit adequacy judgment and established costs under the separate economic gate. There is no source-count route around that judgment.

Write concise `valuation.md` with reasoning, arithmetic/sensitivity, what could change the conclusion and human-review questions. Write at most three evidence-backed lessons in `lessons.md`. Do not edit shared instructions. Mechanical validation checks integrity, references, declarations and numerical bounds; it does not certify source facts or market value.
